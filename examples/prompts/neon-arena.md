# Example prompt — neon alien arena

> Copy this into `node pipeline/bridge.js newgame "<prompt>"`, or paste it as the
> user message in any AI chat whose system prompt is `pipeline/system_prompt.md`
> and import the JSON with `--spec`.

```
A futuristic neon alien wave shooter — the radical departure test. The grid is
infected; purge it in neon. Fast, arena-style rounds, no win condition; the
leaderboard is the goal.

Mood: cyberpunk night — black glass floors, purple ambient, cyan accent light,
heavy atmospheric haze with a glow. Style: neon (glass and light). UI: cyan
tokens on deep violet panels, magenta danger color.

Enemies are glitching machines: sentry drones, fast glitch stalkers, overload
brutes, and a massive core boss every 4 rounds. Their eyes glow cyan; they move
like corrupted code.

Weapons: an energy sidearm, a plasma SMG, a rail shotgun, a pulse rifle; the
wonder weapon is a black-hole projector from the mystery boxes. High fire rates,
low recoil, sci-fi feel — reloads are fast.

Economy: kill rewards flow fast so the pace stays frantic; doors are cheap-ish
but the map still gates into 4 zones. Perks are experimental serums, pack-a-punch
is the overclocker. Balance for a fast, stylish loop — rounds should be punchy,
not grindy.
```
